FAQ - General SIH 2026
Q. 1. What is Smart India Hackathon (SIH)?
SIH is a national innovation competition where students build solutions for real-world problems provided by government and industry organisations.

Q. 2. Who can participate in SIH?
Students from eligible Indian colleges can participate by forming a team and qualifying through their college's Internal Hackathon.

Q. 3. Can first-year students participate?
Yes. First-year students are eligible if they meet the competition guidelines.

Q. 4. How do I register for SIH?
Participate in your college's Internal Hackathon. Selected teams are registered by the college on the SIH portal.

Q. 5. What is the SIH Internal Hackathon?
It is the college-level selection round where teams compete for nomination to SIH.

Q. 6. How do I choose the right problem statement?
Pick one that matches your team's skills, interests, and can be developed into a practical solution.

Q. 7. What skills should an SIH team have?
A balanced team should include coding, UI/UX, problem-solving, presentation, and project management skills.

Q. 8. What do judges look for?
Judges evaluate innovation, technical quality, feasibility, impact, scalability, and presentation.

Q. 9. How are teams selected for the Grand Finale?
Experts review submissions and shortlist the best teams for each problem statement.

Q. 10. How competitive is SIH?
Very competitive, with thousands of teams participating and only a small percentage reaching the finals.

Q. 11. How many teammates do we need to participate?
Each team must consist of 6 members, and at least 1 female member is compulsory.


FAQ - Technical & Strategy
Q. 12. Can we use ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, or open-source libraries?
Yes, but your team must understand the solution and ensure the work is original and follows the rules.

Q. 13. Do we have to build everything from scratch?
No. You can use frameworks, APIs, and libraries while creating an original solution.

Q. 14. What happens during the Grand Finale?
Teams build or refine their prototype, present it to judges, and answer technical questions.

Q. 15. What should we include in our final presentation?
Explain the problem, your solution, architecture, demo, impact, and future improvements.

Q. 16. What questions do SIH judges usually ask?
They ask about your solution's innovation, technology, scalability, security, challenges, and implementation.

Q. 17. What are the biggest mistakes first-time SIH teams make?
Poor planning, weak teamwork, incomplete prototypes, ignoring the problem statement, and poor presentations.

Q. 18. Is SIH only for expert programmers?
No. Teams also need research, design, communication, and presentation skills.

Q. 19. Can beginners qualify for SIH?
Yes. A strong understanding of the problem and a working prototype matter more than experience alone.

Q. 20. Does participating in SIH help with placements?
Yes. It strengthens your résumé by showcasing technical, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Q. 21. Is winning SIH more about the idea or the execution?
Execution matters more. A working, practical solution is usually valued over a good idea alone.
